Privacy

Privacy policy

What we collect, why we keep it, and the control you have over it.

What we collect

To register you for a run, we collect your name and email address. That's all a registration needs.

If you create an account, we also store your email address and a securely hashed version of your password — never the password itself.

If you add your finish time for a run you've done, we store it, so your dashboard can show it.

Why we use it

We use your name and email to confirm your run, to tell you if a run you registered for is called off, and to show you your upcoming and past runs when you're signed in. Those emails are transactional messages about a run you chose to join — not marketing. We won't send you marketing email without asking first.

Public run results

Once a run has taken place, anyone can see its results. Everyone registered for it is listed by first name and last-name initial only (for example “Ana P.”), with the finish time they entered, or “Time not entered” until they add one. Your email address and full surname are never shown.

We do this so runners can compare times — it's part of taking part in a timed run. Deleting your account removes the runs you registered for while signed in from their results.

Cookies

We set two cookies. Both are strictly necessary for the site to work:

  • A security cookie (XSRF-TOKEN), for every visitor. It holds a random value that proves a form was sent from this site. It stops other websites from sending forms in your name.
  • A session cookie (JSESSIONID), only when you're signed in. It keeps you logged in as you move between pages.

Neither cookie identifies you to anyone else or follows you to other sites, so they need no consent banner. We use no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. If that ever changes, we'll ask for your consent first.

Your rights

When you're signed in, your dashboard lets you:

  • Download your data — your account and every run you've registered for, including any finish times, as a file.
  • Delete your account — this permanently removes your account and all your registrations and finish times.

Go to your dashboard to do either under “Your data”.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored in the European Union, encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. We keep it only as long as you have an account; deleting your account erases it.

The site and its database run on Microsoft Azure in an EU region. Confirmation emails are sent through Azure Communication Services, with its data kept in Europe. Microsoft processes this data only on our behalf, to run the site and send those emails.
