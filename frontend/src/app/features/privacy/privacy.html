Privacy

Privacy policy

What we collect, why we keep it, and the control you have over it.

What we collect

To register you for a run, we collect your name and email address. That's all a registration needs.

If you create an account, we also store your email address and a securely hashed version of your password — never the password itself.

Why we use it

We use your name and email to confirm your run and to show you your upcoming and past runs when you're signed in. Your email confirmation is a transactional message about a run you chose to join — not marketing. We won't send you marketing email without asking first.

Cookies

We set one cookie, and only when you're signed in: a session cookie that keeps you logged in as you move between pages. It's strictly necessary for the site to work, so it needs no consent banner. We use no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. If that ever changes, we'll ask for your consent first.

Your rights

When you're signed in, your dashboard lets you:

  • Download your data — your account and every run you've registered for, as a file.
  • Delete your account — this permanently removes your account and all your registrations.

Go to your dashboard to do either under “Your data”.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored in the European Union, encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. We keep it only as long as you have an account; deleting your account erases it.
